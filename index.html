Etsy Niche Intelligence
About the project
Independent marketplace research project

Smarter research before launching on Etsy.

Etsy Niche Intelligence is a personal software project in development. Its goal is to help evaluate marketplace opportunities using transparent keyword evidence, listing competition, pricing and product research.

● Currently in development — not yet a live research service

Planned research modules

The initial scope is focused on personal research for a future Etsy shop, including digital downloads and print-on-demand products.

Planned

Keyword opportunity research

Compare relevant keyword evidence and distinguish third-party search estimates from Etsy marketplace data.

Planned

Listing competition analysis

Review publicly available listing attributes, pricing and market saturation where permitted by data-source terms.

Planned

Tag and title research

Study relevant product tags and title patterns to identify opportunities for clearer listing descriptions.

Planned

Custom difficulty and profit models

Develop explainable, independently designed competition indicators and print-on-demand margin estimates.

Data integrity: This project will label estimates and unavailable metrics clearly. It does not claim access to official Etsy keyword search volumes or a proprietary Etsy ranking score.

Project status

This website is a public project overview. The research application, integrations, and data-validation methods are under development and are not yet offered to external users.

Future API use is intended to follow Etsy's developer policies and applicable rate limits. This independent project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Etsy or Printify.
